Welcome

Your adventure starts the moment you contact us

Dismass Kilimanjaro Experience is a fully licensed Tanzania tour operator based in Arusha, specialising in guided Kilimanjaro climbs, wildlife safaris across the Northern Circuit, and bespoke Zanzibar beach extensions. With over a decade of expertise and a team of certified mountain guides, we deliver safe, memorable, and deeply personal adventures.

Whether you are a first-time trekker or a seasoned expedition traveller, we craft each itinerary around you — your pace, your goals, your story.
